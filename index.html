Every Legami erasable pen, in one place

A collector's catalogue to discover every pen and keep track of your own collection.

I'm a stationery lover who started collecting Legami erasable pens at the end of 2024, and soon wanted one place to see them all.

So I made this catalogue: every pen, pack and special collection, with limited editions marked.

Sign in to tick the pens you own, see how much of the collection you have, and find the ones you're still missing, on any device.

Enjoying the site?
